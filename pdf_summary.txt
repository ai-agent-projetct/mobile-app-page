 Billing System Architecture
Overview
The Billing System manages the complete financial lifecycle of every consultation.
It determines the doctor's consultation fee, applies configurable business rules,
generates a transparent invoice, processes payments securely, records financial
transactions, calculates settlement, and completes doctor payouts.
1. Pricing Engine
Determines the doctor's base consultation fee.
 Retrieve doctor consultation fee.
 Validate consultation type.
 Validate appointment type.
 Return base consultation fee.
2. Billing Engine
Calculates the final payable amount.
 Apply platform fee.
 Calculate GST.
 Apply gateway fee.
 Apply discounts/coupons.
 Calculate grand total.
3. Invoice Engine
Generates the invoice before payment.
 Generate invoice number.
 Create billing breakdown.
 Freeze invoice.
 Generate PDF.

 Record payment.

INTRODUCTION
1. Executive Overview
The Super Admin Panel is proposed as the single, centralized management and monitoring system for the
multi-speciality hospital. It brings together the Doctor App, Patient App, IVR system, Online Consultation
platform, AI Scribe service, and Payment infrastructure into one coherent control layer — giving hospital
leadership a real-time, end-to-end view of clinical and operational activity.
Rather than requiring administrators to log into separate systems for appointments, billing, doctor management, and
communication, the Super Admin Panel consolidates everything into a single, role-aware interface. This reduces
operational overhead, improves visibility, and strengthens governance across the hospital.
What the Super Admin Can Manage and Monitor
Doctors Patients
Departments Appointments
IVR & Calls Online Consultations
AI Scribe Payments
Revenue Staff / Admins
Roles & Permissions Reports & Analytics
Notifications Hospital Settings
System Health Audit Logs
Core Principle: The Super Admin has full administrative access across every module of the
platform, while all other staff users — receptionists, billing staff, department admins, and doctors
— receive access strictly based on their assigned role. This ensures both operational flexibility for
the hospital and strong data governance for sensitive clinical and financial information.

EXCEPTION HANDLING
12. Alerts & Notifications
The dashboard surfaces only alerts that require action — keeping the Super Admin focused on real issues
rather than routine noise.
⚠ Pending payments above threshold
⚠ Failed AI Scribe sessions
⚠ Payment service unavailable
⚠ IVR failures
⚠ Video consultation failures
⚠ Doctors with scheduling conflicts
⚠ High appointment cancellation rate
Guiding principle: Only actionable alerts appear on the main dashboard. Informational or routine
events remain inside their respective modules, so the dashboard never becomes cluttered with
unnecessary notifications.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
26. Key Product Principle
Dashboard = Summary + Trends + Problems + Quick Actions 
Detailed information should always remain inside individual modules — the dashboard exists to summarize,
highlight trends, surface problems, and enable quick action, never to replace the underlying detail views.
Dashboard: 342 appointments today example ↓
Appointments Module: full appointment list with patient, doctor, department, date, time, type, status, and filters.
This keeps the dashboard clean and scannable, while still giving the Super Admin complete control over every
aspect of hospital operations, one click away.
